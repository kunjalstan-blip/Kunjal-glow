MY SKIN - Kunjal's skincare app (free hosting guide)

Files: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, reminders.ics, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, .nojekyll
No build step, no server code.

HOSTING - GitHub Pages (free)
Repo: https://github.com/kunjalstan-blip/Kunjal-glow
1. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save.
2. After about a minute it's live at https://kunjalstan-blip.github.io/Kunjal-glow/

ON YOUR IPHONE
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Share > Add to Home Screen > Add. (Keeps your history safe and opens full screen.)
3. Open My Skin from the Home Screen > Me tab > set your reminder times > "Add reminders to Calendar" > Add All.

NOTES
- Ticks, history and photos are saved on your phone only. Use Me > "Save a backup file" now and then.
- The plan starts Mon 23 Nov 2026. Change it in the Me tab if you start later; every week's products shift with it.
- Product photos load from W cosmetics' website. If one ever fails, a drawing shows instead. You can also tap a product > "Use my own photo".
- To update the app later, re-upload index.html.

USING IT
- Today: swipe the week strip or tap a day. Switch Morning / Night at the top.
- "Start step by step" walks you through each product full screen: photo, amount, how-to, where it goes. Tap "Done, next".
- Or tick the circles in the list. Tap any product for its how-to.
- Follows your iPhone's light / dark mode.
